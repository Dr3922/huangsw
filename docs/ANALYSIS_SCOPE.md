# Analysis scope and claim boundaries

The release supports the following manuscript-facing analyses:

1. cohort eligibility and probe-to-gene processing audits;
2. cohort-internal bulk module scoring and upper-quartile FGFR3 contrasts;
3. context-adjusted bulk models and random-effects meta-analysis;
4. cBioPortal release-level mutation/CNA enumeration with overlap flags;
5. epithelial-compartment single-nucleus pseudobulk and donor-cluster bootstrap;
6. strict leave-one-patient-out spatial projection;
7. Round 2 spatial score-component ablation and minimal composition sensitivity;
8. figure source-data generation.

The spatial scores are RNA-score patterns at spot or neighborhood level. They are not
direct cell counts, protein measurements, functional HLA-I assays, patient-level spatial
replicates, or causal evidence. The single-nucleus compartment is described as epithelial
because definitive malignant labels were not available.
