# Incremental association beyond atlas differentiation

Nested baseline: luminal score (excludes FGFR3 and APM/IFNG genes) plus published state; full model adds standardized FGFR3. Inference clusters by 25 donors with t24; partial R² is in-sample descriptive improvement, not externally validated prediction. For normalized count pseudobulk and >=20 nuclei, 104 donor-state rows remain. APM and combined transcription modules retain negative FGFR3 coefficients; IFNG execution is weaker and its CI crosses zero. See atlas_incremental_nested_models.csv. These findings support incremental RNA association in this atlas. They do not establish a new mechanism. Spatial increment is conditional, heterogeneous and mathematically coupled to the APM-containing projection; signature-only comparisons must accompany it.
