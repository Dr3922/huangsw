# Spatial dependence

Moran I uses directed nonself k=6 equal-weight neighbors: n/S0 sum(wij zi zj)/sum(zi²), S0=6n. Descriptive observed statistics, without an IID permutation P, are in spatial_moran_autocorrelation.csv. Positive spatial clustering and smoothing require spatially aware uncertainty. The executed grid-block bootstrap resamples paired observations within full spatial blocks, 1200 draws at widths 3 and 6 median nonself-neighbor distances, for k=4/6/8. It estimates section-conditional uncertainty, not a patient-level null. No constrained permutation null has been run or claimed. Biological n remains four. Distances are full-resolution image pixels. No tissue-component or barrier masks exist; nearest neighbors may cross gaps. Boundary spots use their nearest available within-section spots; no cross-section edges.
